1. 👤 Profile Pictures (profile_1.png, etc.)
Aspect Ratio: 1:1 (Perfect Square)
Best Dimensions: 500 x 500 pixels (or 400 x 400 px)
Why: Your CSS forces them into a 150x150px circle/square. 
Uploading a 500x500px image ensures it looks super sharp even on high-resolution Retina displays 
(like iPhones and MacBooks).


2. 🌆 Hero Banner (hero_banner.png / banner_video.mp4)
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (Standard Widescreen)
Best Dimensions: 1920 x 1080 pixels (Full HD)
Why: This is the massive background image/video at the top of the dashboard. 
It needs to look cinematic. If you want it to load slightly faster on slower connections,
you can compress it to 1280 x 720 pixels (720p).


3. 📸 Row Posters (The Memory & Trip Cards)
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (Standard Widescreen)
Best Dimensions: 1280 x 720 pixels (720p HD)
Why: On the dashboard itself, these cards shrink down to 260 x 146 px. 
However, because you have the Full-Screen Image Modal feature (when you click a picture, it opens up huge), 
you must use high-quality images like 1280x720. The browser will automatically scale them down for the row, 
but they will look stunning when your partner clicks to expand them!


Quick Tip for Videos 🎬
If you are adding hover videos (hover_video_1.mp4), keep them short (3-5 seconds) and export them at 720p (1280x720) 
or even lower like 480p (854x480). Since they only play inside the small 260x146px box on hover, 
making the video files smaller will ensure the dashboard loads instantly without lagging.
